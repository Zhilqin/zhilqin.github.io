## About Me

Hi, I am Zhilun Qin (秦智伦). I will join the [Shanghai Research Institute for Intelligent Autonomous Systems (SRIAS)](https://srias.tongji.edu.cn/) at [Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/) as a Ph.D. student in Fall 2027.

My research will focus on autonomous robotic systems that can localize accurately and build maps efficiently in real time. I am particularly interested in using 3D computer vision to develop visual perception capabilities that work in real robotic systems. My broader goal is to contribute to spatial intelligence and physical AI.

I am completing my undergraduate degree in Transportation Engineering at Tongji University's [College of Transportation Engineering](https://tjjt.tongji.edu.cn/English_Home/HOME.htm), with graduation expected in 2027. Early in my studies, I focused on intelligent vehicles and intelligent transportation systems. My interests have gradually shifted toward how machines understand the three-dimensional world around them and develop intelligence through interaction with their environments.

I am beginning preliminary research on efficient online mapping for underwater robots and dynamic updates to 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) representations. This work is my first step toward my doctoral research, and I look forward to the work ahead.

## Research Interests

- **Robotic localization and mapping:** efficient SLAM and online mapping in real time.
- **3D computer vision and visual perception:** 3D scene understanding for autonomous robots.
- **Spatial intelligence and physical AI:** understanding and interacting with the physical world.
